⚛ How to Play

The target word exists in quantum superposition of TWO 5-letter words. Deduce both to achieve decoherence!

TILE COLOURS

AGreen — Correct position. If unique to one word, triggers collapse.
AYellow — Letter in both words, wrong spot.
APurple — Letter in one word only, wrong spot.
AGray — Not in either word.

PHASES

  • Superposition: Guess against two hidden words.
  • Collapsed: A unique green collapses reality — solve that word.
  • Alternate Reality: First word solved — now find the other.

THE PARADOX RULE

If a single guess has unique greens for both words, they cancel out! No collapse — those tiles turn Purple instead.
